# Changelog

What changed in each release of EasyPDF. The release workflow puts a version's section in the
notes of its GitHub release, so every version tagged needs one here.

## 1.1.1 - 2026-10-26

- One page or a grid of pages at a time, each notch of the mouse wheel turns a page (or a
  sheet) at once, however tall it is and however fast the wheel spins. Before, the wheel first
  scrolled down a page taller than the window, and turned no more than one page every 180 ms.
  A touchpad still scrolls through a tall page before turning to the next.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-10-24

- 3D models: clicking a 3D annotation in PRC (EPLAN's cabinet layouts, for one) shows its
  model over the pages, to turn, move and zoom, from any of the views stored with it - an
  EPLAN model has one for every device. U3D models are not shown yet.

## 1.0.3 - 2026-10-23

- The EasyPDF icon on the taskbar and in the Start menu is no longer blank.

## 1.0.2 - 2026-10-17

- The header's buttons, boxes and icons are centred in the strip that shows, and typed search
  text and the page number sit on the same line as the text beside them.

## 1.0.1 - 2026-10-15

- Automatic updates: EasyPDF looks for a new release once a day, checks the download, and
  installs it when EasyPDF closes, or at once with Update. Install this version by hand once;
  later ones come by themselves.
- The trail of jumps holds each page once.
- Several pages at a time, the arrow keys, Page Up/Down and Space turn the whole sheet;
  Ctrl+Up/Down scroll within a tall one.
- The outline and page thumbnails keep their scroll bar beside the rows, not over them.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-15

- The first release.
